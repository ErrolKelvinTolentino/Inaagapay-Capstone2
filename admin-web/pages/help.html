Frequently asked questions
Common guidance for portal users.
Why can’t I see a patient or facility?

The portal only shows records covered by your assigned office. If the record should be visible, contact your Municipal Health Office administrator.

How do I update my password?

Open the profile menu, choose Settings, then select Change password.

What should I do if my session expires?

Sign in again using your authorized MHO or RHU credentials. The timeout helps protect sensitive health information.
