Access & navigation

Finding pages and understanding office access.

Why can’t I see a patient or facility?

The portal only shows records covered by your assigned office. If the record should be visible, ask your Municipal Health Office administrator to verify the facility assignment.

Where can I find my profile and settings?

Open your name in the top-right account menu. From there, you can open your Profile, Settings, or this Help & Support page.

Records & workflows

Handling unexpected or incorrect operational data.

What should I do if information looks incorrect?

Confirm that you are viewing the correct patient, facility, and date range. Do not create a duplicate record. Escalate the issue through your approved internal support channel if you cannot correct it through the normal workflow.

How can I check who changed a record?

Open the Audit Trail and search by record, staff member, module, or date. Select View Note to review the recorded event details available to your account.

Account & security

Password, session, and privacy guidance.

How do I update my password?

Open Settings and choose Change password. Use a password that is unique to InaAgapay and do not share it with another staff member.

Change password
What should I do if my session expires?

Sign in again with your authorized MHO or RHU credentials. The portal signs you out after inactivity to protect sensitive health information on unattended devices.

Escalation & privacy

Report issues without exposing patient information

If a problem blocks patient care, prevents authorized access, or could affect data accuracy, contact your office’s authorized administrator through the approved internal support channel.

  1. Record the page name, approximate time, and the action you were attempting.
  2. Describe the error message without copying passwords, tokens, or patient clinical details.
  3. Do not send patient names, record screenshots, or exported reports through unapproved messaging services.
Privacy reminder Use only the minimum information needed to identify and resolve the issue.
