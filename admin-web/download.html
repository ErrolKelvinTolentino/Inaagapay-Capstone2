InaAgapay Staff portal
For Android phones

Your pregnancy and your baby's health, in one app.

InaAgapay keeps your checkups, vaccines and your child's growth in your pocket, and reminds you before every visit to your health center.

Ang iyong pagbubuntis at kalusugan ng iyong anak, nasa isang app.

Download for Android
Version Android 8.0 or newer Free

Who it is for

Mothers

Your midwife creates your account at the health center, or you can register with your mobile number.

  • Checkup, ultrasound and lab results as your midwife records them
  • Reminders before each checkup and vaccine
  • Your child's growth, vaccines and baby book
  • Danger signs to watch for, in English and Filipino

Midwives

Sign in with the account your RHU created for you.

  • Record checkups, ultrasounds, lab tests, Td and child vaccines
  • See who is due, overdue or high-risk at a glance
  • Manage your health center's vaccine and medicine stock
  • Export records and statistics to PDF and Excel

How to install

  1. Tap "Download for Android". The file is called inaagapay.apk. Wait for it to finish. I-tap ang "Download for Android" at hintaying matapos.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This only allows apps you choose to open. Kung magtanong ang phone, payagan ang browser na mag-install ng app.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. Sign in with the email or mobile number and password your health center gave you. Mag-sign in gamit ang account na ibinigay ng iyong health center.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone.

The code opens this page on your phone so you can download the app there.

Only install InaAgapay from this page or from your health center. If anyone sends you the app another way, ask your midwife first.
