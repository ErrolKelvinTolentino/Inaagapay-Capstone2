Two sides of the same care

What your midwife records at the health center is what you see at home.

For mothers

Your midwife sets up your account at the health center, or you can register with your mobile number.

  • Every record your midwife saves, the moment she saves it
  • Reminders before check-ups and vaccines
  • Your child's growth charts, vaccines and baby book
  • Save or share your records as PDF

For midwives

Sign in with the account your Rural Health Unit created for you.

  • Record check-ups, ultrasounds, lab tests, Td and child vaccines
  • See who is due, overdue or high-risk at a glance
  • Run your health center's vaccine and medicine stock
  • Export records and statistics to PDF and Excel

Install in three steps

1

Download the app

Tap Get InaAgapay to download inaagapay.apk. Your browser chooses the save location, usually Downloads.

I-tap ang "Get InaAgapay" at hintaying matapos.

2

Open the file

Open inaagapay.apk from your browser's Downloads or Files > Downloads. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.

Kung magtanong ang phone, payagan ang browser na mag-install.

3

Install and sign in

Tap Install, then Open. Sign in with the account from your health center.

Mag-sign in gamit ang account mula sa iyong health center.
